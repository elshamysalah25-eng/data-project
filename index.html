Our story

A brand built on handcrafted beauty and heartfelt gifting.

Cakes by Shayma began with one vision: to create cakes that speak softly but leave a lasting impression. Each piece is shaped by hand, layered with premium ingredients, and finished with couture-level detail for Egypt’s most meaningful celebrations.

From intimate birthday surprises to lavish wedding centerpieces, we honor every order with warmth, patience, and artistry. Our clients trust us for consistency, elevated taste, and a romantic aesthetic that feels modern and feminine without exclusivity.
